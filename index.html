What it does

Hearing your own voice slightly delayed can help you slow down and find a steadier rhythm while you speak. Cadence does this with your own voice, nothing else. A gate mutes playback when you are not speaking, so traffic and construction noise are not played back. Your voice is never recorded. It is played back live and discarded.

  • Delay from 40 to 200 ms, in 10 ms steps.
  • Only your voice: a sensitivity slider sets how easily the gate opens.
  • Fading program: lowers the delay in steps you choose.
  • Runs in the background, with a notification you can use with the screen off.
  • Progress: a daily goal and a calendar of your sessions. Optional daily reminder.

Install

You need earbuds with a microphone. Wired earbuds are recommended, because Bluetooth adds a much longer delay on top of the one you set. Open the APK on your phone and follow these steps:

  1. Download the APK with the button above.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager.
  3. Tap Install, then open Cadence.
